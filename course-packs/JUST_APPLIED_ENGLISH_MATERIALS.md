# JUST Applied Academic & Professional English 2026–27 — Teaching Materials

This folder's source of truth is:

`just-applied-english-2026-27.coursepack.json`

It implements the approved **15-session Term 1 + 19-session Term 2** architecture. Term 2 contains three removable FLEX sessions: T2.13, T2.15 and T2.16.

## Phase I material layer

Every one of the 34 lessons now contains:

- a linked session-specific **lesson kit** in EduBoard Resources;
- an original input, case, dataset, dialogue or source set;
- a student-facing task sheet inside that kit;
- explicit **Support / Core / Stretch** differentiation;
- an eight-stage Production-Oriented lesson flow:
  1. retrieval;
  2. output challenge;
  3. targeted noticing/input;
  4. guided practice;
  5. production 1;
  6. feedback;
  7. improved attempt;
  8. exit ticket;
- a short homework/retrieval/portfolio continuation where appropriate;
- the shared 90-minute POA spine;
- the shared Support/Core/Stretch guide.

The Course Pack also includes the reusable **University communication activity bank**.

## PowerPoint workflow

EduBoard already exports a lesson plan to an editable PPTX.

The Phase I lesson flows are deliberately written so that export produces:

- title slide;
- objectives;
- materials;
- one slide per activity stage;
- homework/follow-up.

This keeps the real curriculum data in EduBoard rather than maintaining a second set of disconnected PowerPoint files. The generated deck remains editable for visual refinement, local images, institution branding and last-minute class-specific changes.

## Student materials

The lesson kit note attached to each lesson contains the core student material:

- original reading/listening/case/data input;
- task instructions;
- Support scaffolds;
- Stretch extension;
- feedback/exit prompt.

These can be projected, copied into a handout, or adapted into the teacher's normal delivery format.

## Content provenance

The Phase I material layer is newly authored for the approved 2026–27 Course Pack.

An earlier v02 JUST resource ecosystem used a **28-week** structure and demonstrated a useful resource architecture (student resource, teacher guide, PPT outline, assessment/evidence and offline-ready materials). That earlier sequence is **not** the current curriculum source of truth and must not be used to overwrite the 15+19 plan.

## Safety / calibration boundaries

- Classroom datasets and organisational scenarios labelled fictional are teaching simulations, not claims about JUST or local companies.
- Assessment difficulty should still be calibrated after the Session 1 diagnostic.
- Institutional requirements such as official weighting, required textbook, device rules or formal syllabus remain external decision gates until confirmed.
- AI activities require the student to verify claims and retain responsibility for the final output.
- The course remains applied communication, not a generic CET preparation course.

## Regression controls

Tests enforce that:

- the pack remains valid against the current Course Pack schema;
- the 15+19 structure and FLEX sessions remain intact;
- all nine UENG Standards remain present;
- all 34 planned lessons have a matching lesson kit;
- every lesson retains the POA spine and Support/Core/Stretch resources;
- every lesson has exactly eight structured activity stages;
- the complete real Course Pack imports and re-imports into a temporary SQLite database without curriculum duplication.
