# Demo video script (about 2½ minutes)

For a school's head, a teacher, or an investor who has never seen EduBoard. Everything
shown is real and can be recorded from the **sample school** in the app (Settings → Help
and updates → Sample school) and the **Portal demo login** (`demo` / `try-eduboard`).
No real student appears anywhere.

**Before recording:** the name "EduBoard" may change (see `NAME_AND_LICENCE.md`). The
script says the name twice, in scenes 1 and 9, so a rename means re-recording just those.

**Setup:** open the sample school fresh (Start over). Record at 1920×1080 with the app
window at about 1280×800, and the Portal on a phone or in a browser's phone view
(390 px wide). Turn on "Show the class average" and "Include my comments on scores" in
Settings → Portal and families, then publish once, if you record your own Portal instead
of the demo.

**Recording the Chinese version:** switch the app to 中文 (bottom of the sidebar) and the
Portal to 中文 (top right), and read the Chinese lines. The shots are the same.

## Shorter cut (45 seconds, for social media)

Scenes 2, 3 (first half), 6 and 9, with on-screen captions instead of a voice:

1. "Who needs attention today?" / “今天该关注谁？”
2. "Marks in, grades out." / “录入分数，自动出成绩。”
3. "Families see their own child's progress." / “家长看到自己孩子的进步。”
4. "Try it free: edu-board.com" / “免费试用：edu-board.com”

## Things not to show or say

- Real student names or any school's real data: use only the sample school and demo.
- Promises not yet true: no "certified", "audited", "used by N schools", or "secure
  like a bank". The security overview lists what isn't done yet.
- AI writing report cards by itself. It only suggests; the teacher checks every one.
