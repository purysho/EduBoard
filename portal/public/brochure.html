EduBoardFor schools

Teachers keep their records. Families see their own child's progress.

EduBoard is a free app for a teacher's gradebook, attendance and reports, with a Portal where students and families see their own work, grades and messages on any phone. In English and Chinese.

For teachers

  • A gradebook with categories, weights, rubrics and the school's grading scale; import scores from a spreadsheet.
  • Attendance with the school's own codes and QR check-in; seating chart, class points, random picker, timer.
  • Report cards for the whole class at once, with a comment bank and AI suggestions the teacher checks.
  • Homework with self-marking questions, exit tickets, lesson plans, letters home; Word, Excel and PowerPoint export.
  • Works without internet, on Windows, Mac and Linux.

For families

  • Grades with each marked assessment, a chart over time and the teacher's feedback.
  • Homework handed in online, and due dates in the family's own calendar.
  • Report cards as PDFs, notices with reply slips (回执), and messages with the teacher, translated when needed.
  • A Study Helper that answers from the teacher's materials (the teacher sees what's asked).
  • A weekly email summary, and a copy of all their data at any time.

For the school

  • Student records stay on the teacher's computer, encrypted with password protection.
  • The Portal holds only what the teacher publishes: hosted in Hong Kong, or on the school's own server (open source).
  • Families agree to the terms at first sign-in; the school can download the record.
  • A privacy notice, data processing terms and a security overview, ready for review.
  • A usage page: families active each week, report cards opened, notices answered.

EduBoardFor schools

Try it in ten minutes

  1. Download the app at edu-board.com. In Settings → Help and updates, open the Sample school: two classes of made-up students with a term of scores, attendance and reports. Your own data is never touched.
  2. See what families see: edu-board.com → Log in, username demo, password try-eduboard.
  3. For your IT and data protection review: edu-board.com/security, /privacy and /data-processing.

Starting with one class

  • A teacher installs the app and adds a class, or imports a class list.
  • Publishing sends the Portal only what families should see.
  • Families join with an invite code or QR code from the teacher.

Working with China's tools

  • Posts to DingTalk and WeCom class groups.
  • AI through DeepSeek, Qwen or Zhipu, reachable from China.
  • Documents open in WPS as well as Microsoft Office.

Honest about what's next

  • No outside security audit or certification yet.
  • Two-step sign-in and off-site backups for the hosted Portal are planned.
  • See edu-board.com/security for the full list.

EduBoard学校介绍

记录留在老师手里，家长看到自己孩子的进步。

EduBoard 是一款免费的教学应用，帮老师管理成绩册、考勤和报告；配套的学生门户让学生和家长在手机上查看自己的作业、成绩和消息。支持中文和英文。

老师

  • 成绩册：支持成绩类别、权重、评分量规和学校自己的等级标准，可从表格导入成绩。
  • 考勤：使用学校自己的考勤代码和扫码签到；还有座位表、课堂积分、随机点名和计时器。
  • 成绩单：全班一次生成，配有评语库和 AI 短语建议，老师检查后才使用。
  • 作业（含自动批改题）、出门条、教案、家长信，并可导出 Word、Excel 和 PowerPoint。
  • 无需联网即可使用，支持 Windows、Mac 和 Linux。

家长

  • 成绩：每项已评分测评、成绩变化图和老师的反馈。
  • 在线提交作业，截止日期可加入自己的手机日历。
  • PDF 成绩单、带回执的通知，以及与老师的消息（需要时可翻译）。
  • 学习助手：根据老师自己的资料回答问题，老师可以看到提问内容。
  • 每周邮件摘要，随时可下载自己的全部数据。

学校

  • 学生记录保存在老师电脑上，开启密码保护后加密。
  • 学生门户只保存老师发布的内容，服务器位于香港，也可部署在学校自己的服务器上（代码开源）。
  • 家长首次登录时同意使用条款，学校可下载同意记录存档。
  • 隐私说明、数据处理条款和安全概述，便于学校审核。
  • 使用情况页面：每周活跃家庭、成绩单打开率、通知回执率。

EduBoard学校介绍

十分钟试用

  1. 在 edu-board.com 下载应用。在“设置 → 帮助与更新”中打开示例学校：两个班的虚构学生，含一学期的成绩、考勤和报告。不会改动你自己的数据。
  2. 看看家长看到什么：edu-board.com → 登录，用户名 demo，密码 try-eduboard。
  3. 供学校信息部门和数据保护审核：edu-board.com/security、/privacy 和 /data-processing。

从一个班开始

  • 老师安装应用，添加班级或导入学生名单。
  • 发布时只把家长应该看到的内容发送到学生门户。
  • 家长用老师给的邀请码或二维码加入。

适配国内工具

  • 可发布到钉钉和企业微信班级群。
  • AI 可使用 DeepSeek、通义千问或智谱，国内可直接访问。
  • 文档可用 WPS 或 Microsoft Office 打开。

坦诚说明

  • 尚未进行外部安全审计或认证。
  • 托管门户的两步登录和异地备份正在计划中。
  • 完整列表见 edu-board.com/security。
