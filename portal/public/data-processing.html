Data processing terms

For schools using the EduBoard Portal at portal.edu-board.com · Version of 27 September 2026

These terms are between the school named at the end (the School) and the developer of EduBoard (the Provider). They apply when the School's teachers publish to portal.edu-board.com. A school that runs the Portal on its own server does not need them. Print this page or save it as a PDF, fill in the table at the end, sign it and send a copy to privacy@edu-board.com; the Provider signs and returns it. The privacy notice describes the data in more detail and forms part of these terms.

1. Roles

The School decides why and how personal information is processed (it is the controller; 个人信息处理者). The Provider processes it only on the School's behalf (it is the processor; 受托人). The School's instructions are these terms and what its teachers do in EduBoard, such as publishing, removing a student or changing settings.

2. What is processed

Purpose Letting students and families see their own classwork, grades, attendance, messages and shared material, and letting teachers see what was handed in and read.
People The School's students, their parents or guardians, and its teachers.
Information Names, student numbers, dates of birth, class names, grades, attendance rates, class points, report cards (PDF) and when they were opened, homework and answers, uploaded files, messages, Class Story posts and who has seen and replied to them, optional profile details and photo, Study Helper questions and answers, usernames, bcrypt password hashes and optional email addresses.
Sensitive information Information about children, which under China's Personal Information Protection Law is sensitive for children under 14. The School should not publish health or other special information to the Portal (EduBoard does not send its extra student fields or notes there).
Operations Storing, showing to signed-in users, backing up, deleting; sending the weekly digest through the School's own email account; sending Study Helper questions (with a nameless summary of the student's own practice: cards and quick-check questions they keep missing, their week's goal, and the level, goals and hint choice they wrote in their own profile; for a revision plan, also the titles and due dates of homework not handed in yet) and translations to the AI provider the School chose, with the School's own key.
Duration While the School uses portal.edu-board.com, and until deletion under section 8.

3. What the Provider will do

  • Process the information only on the School's instructions, and tell the School if it believes an instruction breaks the law.
  • Never sell it, use it for advertising, profile anyone, or use it to train AI models.
  • Allow only people who need it to keep the service running to reach the database, and bind them to confidentiality.
  • Keep these safeguards in place: HTTPS only; passwords stored as bcrypt hashes; limits on sign-in attempts and secret links; each teacher's data kept apart by their own sync secret; server access by key or password restricted to the Provider; security updates applied; backups on the same server, the oldest deleted once there are 14.
  • Not disclose the information to anyone unless the law requires it, and then tell the School first unless the law forbids that.

4. Sub-processors

The Provider uses:

  • VPS.do (VpsQuan L.L.C.): the server, in Hong Kong, where the Portal runs.
  • Cloudflare: domain name service only. It tells browsers which server to use; personal information does not pass through it.

The AI provider and email account the School sets up in EduBoard are used with the School's own key and account, under the School's own agreement with them; they are not the Provider's sub-processors. The Provider will tell the School at least 30 days before adding or replacing a sub-processor. The School may object, and if the objection cannot be resolved, may stop using the service and have its data deleted.

5. Security incidents

If personal information of the School's is lost, disclosed or accessed without permission, the Provider will tell the School without undue delay and within 48 hours of finding out, say what is known (what happened, whose information, what is being done), keep the School updated, and help it inform families and the authorities.

6. Helping the School

The Provider will help the School answer requests from students and families to see, correct, copy or delete their information, and give the School what it needs for a personal information protection impact assessment (个人信息保护影响评估) or similar review.

7. Where the information is kept

The information is kept in Hong Kong. Under the Personal Information Protection Law, Hong Kong is outside mainland China. A school in mainland China is responsible for any steps the law requires before sending personal information there (for example a standard contract for cross-border transfer, 个人信息出境标准合同), and the Provider will cooperate, including by signing such a contract. A school elsewhere that needs standard contractual clauses can ask for them in the same way.

8. Ending

When the School stops using portal.edu-board.com, or asks, the Provider will first give the School a copy of its data if it wants one, then delete it from the live Portal within 30 days. Copies in backups are removed as those backups are replaced, and within a further 30 days at most. The Provider will confirm the deletion in writing.

9. Checking

The Provider will answer the School's reasonable questions about how it meets these terms, including a security questionnaire once a year. An inspection by an independent auditor bound by confidentiality can be arranged with 30 days' notice, at the School's cost.

10. Other terms

If these terms conflict with any other agreement between the School and the Provider about this service, these terms win for personal information. They continue for as long as the Provider holds the School's information.

数据处理条款

适用于使用 portal.edu-board.com 学生门户的学校 · 2026 年 9 月 27 日版

本条款由文末列明的学校（下称学校）与 EduBoard 的开发者（下称服务方）订立，适用于学校的老师向 portal.edu-board.com 发布内容的情形。在自己服务器上运行学生门户的学校无需签署。请打印本页或存为 PDF，填写文末表格并签字，然后发送至 privacy@edu-board.com；服务方签字后寄回。隐私说明对数据有更详细的说明，是本条款的一部分。

1. 角色

学校决定个人信息的处理目的和方式（即“个人信息处理者”）。服务方只代表学校处理这些信息（即“受托人”）。学校的指示包括本条款，以及学校的老师在 EduBoard 中的操作，例如发布、删除学生或更改设置。

2. 处理的内容

目的 让学生和家长查看自己的作业、成绩、考勤、消息和分享的资料，让老师看到哪些作业已交、哪些内容已读。
涉及的人 学校的学生、其家长或监护人，以及学校的老师。
信息 姓名、学号、出生日期、班级名称、成绩、出勤率、课堂积分、成绩单（PDF）及其打开时间、作业及答案、上传的文件、消息、班级动态及其阅读和回复情况、可选的个人资料和照片、学习助手的问题和回答、用户名、bcrypt 密码散列以及可选的邮箱地址。
敏感个人信息 儿童的信息：根据《个人信息保护法》，不满十四周岁未成年人的个人信息属于敏感个人信息。学校不应把健康等特殊信息发布到学生门户（EduBoard 不会把自建学生字段或备注发送到学生门户）。
处理方式 存储、向已登录用户展示、备份、删除；通过学校自己的邮箱账号发送每周周报；用学校自己的密钥，把学习助手的问题（附带不含姓名的学生本人练习摘要：经常答错的卡片和快速检测题、本周目标，以及学生在个人资料中自己填写的水平、目标和提示方式；生成复习计划时，还有尚未提交的作业标题和截止日期）和翻译请求发送给学校选择的 AI 服务商。
期限 学校使用 portal.edu-board.com 期间，直至按第 8 条删除为止。

3. 服务方的义务

  • 只按学校的指示处理信息；如认为某项指示违法，告知学校。
  • 绝不出售这些信息，不用于广告或用户画像，也不用于训练 AI 模型。
  • 只允许为维持服务运行而确有需要的人员访问数据库，并要求其承担保密义务。
  • 保持以下保护措施：只使用 HTTPS；以 bcrypt 散列保存密码；限制登录尝试和秘密链接的请求次数；每位老师的数据通过其各自的同步密钥相互隔离；服务器只允许服务方通过密钥或密码访问；及时安装安全更新；备份保存在同一台服务器上，超过 14 份时删除最旧的一份。
  • 除法律要求外不向任何人披露这些信息；如法律要求披露，除法律禁止外，先告知学校。

4. 分包处理方

服务方使用：

  • VPS.do（VpsQuan L.L.C.）：运行学生门户的服务器，位于香港。
  • Cloudflare：仅提供域名解析，告诉浏览器使用哪台服务器；个人信息不经过它。

学校在 EduBoard 中设置的 AI 服务商和邮箱账号，使用的是学校自己的密钥和账号，适用学校与其自行订立的协议，不属于服务方的分包处理方。服务方在增加或更换分包处理方前至少 30 天通知学校。学校可以提出异议；如无法解决，学校可以停止使用本服务并要求删除其数据。

5. 安全事件

如学校的个人信息发生丢失、泄露或未经许可的访问，服务方将及时告知学校，最迟在发现后 48 小时内，说明已知情况（发生了什么、涉及谁的信息、正在采取什么措施），持续向学校通报进展，并协助学校通知家长和主管部门。

6. 协助学校

服务方将协助学校答复学生和家长查阅、更正、复制或删除其信息的请求，并向学校提供开展个人信息保护影响评估或类似评估所需的资料。

7. 信息存放地点

信息存放在香港。根据《个人信息保护法》，香港属于境外。中国大陆的学校负责完成法律要求的个人信息出境手续（例如订立个人信息出境标准合同），服务方将予以配合，包括签署此类合同。其他地区需要标准合同条款的学校，也可以同样提出。

8. 终止

学校停止使用 portal.edu-board.com 或提出要求时，服务方先按学校需要提供一份数据副本，然后在 30 天内从正在运行的学生门户中删除。备份中的副本会随备份更替而删除，最迟再过 30 天。服务方将书面确认删除。

9. 核查

服务方将答复学校关于其如何履行本条款的合理询问，包括每年一次的安全问卷。经 30 天通知，可以安排受保密义务约束的独立审计师进行检查，费用由学校承担。

10. 其他

如本条款与学校和服务方之间关于本服务的其他协议相冲突，涉及个人信息的事项以本条款为准。只要服务方仍持有学校的信息，本条款持续有效。
